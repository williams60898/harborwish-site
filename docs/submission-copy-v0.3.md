## About  (/about/)

Page title: About Harborwish | Harborwish

Meta description: Harborwish is a harbor you can always come back to: 64 mood animals, each a different way of feeling. Learn what it is and who makes it.

H1: A harbor you can always come back to

Most personality tests ask who you are. Harborwish asks something gentler: who are you right now?

Personality is your climate; emotion is today’s weather. Harborwish is about the weather: the mood you’re actually in today, with a name and a picture you can recognize yourself in. We use 64 mood animals because a feeling is easier to hold when it has a face.

### Where it comes from

Harborwish is built on a six-dimension model of how a person feels at a given moment: valence, energy, agency, social approach or avoidance, time orientation, and clarity. Each dimension draws on established ideas in emotion psychology, such as the pleasure–arousal–dominance model, approach–avoidance motivation, time-perspective research, and constructed-emotion theory. The way we combine them, and the 64 mood animals, are our own.

We present Harborwish as a tool for self-reflection. It is not a scientific or clinical instrument, and we don’t claim it measures you.

The framework is written up in a book manuscript, *Who Are You Right Now*.

### What Harborwish isn’t

Harborwish is not a medical or psychological service. It can’t diagnose anything, and nothing on this site is medical, psychological, legal, or financial advice.

If you are struggling, please reach out to a qualified professional or someone you trust. If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services or a crisis line in your country.

### Who we are

Harborwish™ is our trademark. The animal illustrations on this site are created with the help of AI. Our company details are on the Contact page.
